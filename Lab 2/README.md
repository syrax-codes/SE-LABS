#### Lab 2 – Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira ####

- This lab focuses on converting the functional requirements from Lab 1 into Epics and User Stories using Jira. The backlog was prioritized and estimated using Story Points, followed by the planning and simulation of two sprints.

- The project's topic is a Remote Patient Vitals Alert & Monitoring App, focusing on patient vital monitoring, anomaly detection, emergency alerts, caregiver response, and system security and reliability.

- The lab also includes analyzing Sprint Boards and Burndown Charts to understand sprint progress and team capacity.
